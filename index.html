01 Why nobody touches the landing page

Nobody's neglecting the page. They can't reach it.

Your media buyer can spin up a new ad by lunchtime. The page is a different animal. A dev ticket, a designer to brief, and a queue behind whatever the ecom manager already committed to this sprint. Trying something new there costs a fortnight and three people's goodwill.

So the team optimises what it can reach. Sensibly. And a year goes by where the ads have been through eighty iterations and the page has been through none.

Ad creative decides whether someone clicks. The page decides whether they buy.

Your team has been iterating hard on the cheaper half of that equation, because it's the half they can actually touch.
